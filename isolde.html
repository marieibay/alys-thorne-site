Isolde

The Shadow · The Protector

IsoldeThe Shadow · The Protector
The Shadow. The Protector. The Weapon Turned Rebel.

Isolde once moved through the city like a ghost — silent, feared, and bound by blood-oaths to the Vampire Council. For nearly a century, she carried out their will with brutal efficiency… until the night she disobeyed. Faced with orders to execute an entire hybrid bloodline, she vanished instead, earning the Council's eternal wrath and a bounty on her head. For years, she survived in exile — alone, hunted, and haunted by the weight of what she'd done… and what she refused to do.

When she crossed paths with Tessa Cole, Isolde expected nothing but trouble. What she found was something far more dangerous: hope. Against every instinct, she chose to stay — first to protect Tessa, then to fight beside her, and finally, to love her. Cold and controlled on the surface, Isolde is a study in contradictions: ruthless yet tender, hardened yet deeply loyal. She struggles to forgive herself for her past but refuses to let Tessa walk into the fire alone.

Wielding twin daggers and a mind sharpened by survival, Isolde remains Tessa's fiercest ally — and the one person willing to burn the world down with her, if it means carving a new one from the ashes.
