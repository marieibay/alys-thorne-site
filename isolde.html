Isolde

The Shadow · Rogue Vampire Protector

IsoldeThe Shadow · Rogue Vampire Protector
The Lethal Blade. The Rogue Enforcer. The Shadow at Her Back.

Isolde was once the Council's most lethal weapon — a shadow that executed orders without hesitation or remorse. Cold, precise, and feared across Blackspire, she walked the line between predator and monster until an order to butcher innocents broke her allegiance.

Now a traitor marked for death, Isolde's solitary existence is shattered when she intercepts an attack on Tessa Cole. Drawn by an inexplicable instinct and a connection that defies centuries of logic, Isolde becomes Tessa's shield. Beneath her hardened exterior lies a soul capable of fierce, terrifying devotion.

“Cross this threshold, and you answer to my steel.”
