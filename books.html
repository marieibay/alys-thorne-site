Reader FAQ

✦

Everything you need to know before stepping into the shadows of Blackspire.

What order should I read the Veins of Silver series in?

The series is a continuous story best experienced in publication order: start with Book I: Blood Awakens, follow with Book II: Shadows Unbound, and conclude with the finale, Book III: The Blood Remembers.

Is the Veins of Silver trilogy complete?

Yes! The trilogy is complete. Readers can binge the entire story arc from start to finish without waiting for sequels.

Is it available on Kindle Unlimited?

Yes, all three books are currently enrolled in Kindle Unlimited, meaning active subscribers can read the entire trilogy at no additional cost. Paperbacks and hardcovers are also available on Amazon.

What romance pairings and tropes does the series have?

The romance is a slow-burn sapphic (FF) relationship between Tessa Cole, an ordinary mortal whose rare hybrid blood awakens, and Isolde, a rogue vampire enforcer. Key tropes include enemies-to-lovers, bodyguard/protector, forbidden romance, ancient bloodlines, and found family rebellion.

How dark is the fantasy?

The series features gothic urban atmosphere, blood magic, high-stakes violence, political betrayal, and mature themes fitting a dark romantic fantasy world. It is recommended for older young adult and new adult / adult fantasy readers.
