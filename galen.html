Galen

The Rebel Strategist · Shadow of the Rebellion

GalenThe Rebel Strategist
The Architect of Survival. The Tactical Mind Behind the Shadows.

Once a high-ranking tactician in service to the Vampire Council, Galen defected the day he realized the laws he enforced were designed not for peace, but for slaughter. Pragmatic, calculating, and armed with an intricate mental map of Blackspire's power grid and catacombs, he provides the tactical backbone the rebel underground requires.

While Isolde is the razor and Tessa is the flame, Galen is the blueprint. He ensures that when the rebellion strikes, every movement is purposeful and every retreat is secured.

“Every battle is decided before the first blade is drawn.”

Key moments

  • Book ICoordinates safe houses and escape routes when Tessa Cole is marked by the Council.
  • Book IIInfiltrates Council communications to stay one step ahead of the purge.
  • Book IIIOrchestrates the multi-pronged assault on the high spires of Blackspire.
