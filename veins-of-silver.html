Veins of Silver Trilogy

A completed dark romantic fantasy trilogy of forbidden bonds, ancient bloodlines, and rebellion.

Veins of Silver is a completed three-book dark romantic fantasy trilogy by Alys Thorne. Set in Blackspire—a gothic metropolis ruled from high spires by an immortal Vampire Council—the series follows Tessa Cole, an ordinary mortal whose dormant hybrid blood awakens, and Isolde, a rogue vampire enforcer who chooses exile over massacre to protect her.

Series Overview FAQ

✦

Key facts about the Veins of Silver reading experience.

Do I need to read the books in order?

Yes. Veins of Silver is an overarching serialized storyline that follows the same main characters across all three books. Start with Blood Awakens, follow with Shadows Unbound, and finish with The Blood Remembers.

Is the series complete?

Yes, the trilogy is complete and fully published. There are no unresolved cliffhangers awaiting future sequels.

Where can I read the series?

All three books are available on Amazon in eBook, paperback, and hardcover formats, and are included in Kindle Unlimited for subscribers to read for free.
