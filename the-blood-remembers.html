The Blood Remembers
Veins of Silver · III
The Blood Remembers is Book III and the climactic conclusion of the completed dark romantic fantasy trilogy Veins of Silver by Alys Thorne.

Synopsis

The storm is here. With the city on the brink of war and the Hollow stirring beneath their feet, Tessa and Isolde must fight to break the curse that defined their bloodlines. But victory will demand sacrifice — and their love may not survive the fire. In a world where memory is weapon and love is defiance, two hearts may not be enough to change fate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is The Blood Remembers the final book in the series?

Yes. The Blood Remembers concludes the Veins of Silver trilogy, resolving Tessa and Isolde's journey, the rebellion against the Council, and the ancient blood curse. [CONFIRM: Complete trilogy confirmation]
