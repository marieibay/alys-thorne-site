Privacy

Plain-language notes on what this site does with your information.

What this site collects

The site itself does not use accounts, analytics, or advertising cookies. If you join the newsletter, the email address you enter is sent to the email service connected to the signup form so that you can receive updates. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any message.

Third-party services

  • Google Fonts loads the typefaces used on this site.
  • Book images and character portraits are loaded from the author's existing Carrd pages, and the red moon photograph is loaded from Wikimedia Commons.
  • On the Play page, a QR-code image is generated by api.qrserver.com.
  • Links to Amazon, ThriftBooks, Goodreads, Carrd, Blogger and the game site are provided for convenience. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Your choices

You can browse this site without sharing any personal information. To stop newsletter emails, use the unsubscribe link in any message.

Contact

Questions about this page? Use the options on the contact page.
