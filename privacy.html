Privacy Policy

How your information is protected. Plain, transparent, and private.

Overview

This privacy policy explains how Alys Thorne collects, uses, and protects any personal information you provide when using this website.

Information Collection

We only collect personal information that you voluntarily choose to provide to us. When you subscribe to the author newsletter, we collect your name and email address for the sole purpose of sending you book updates, release announcements, and author newsletters.

How We Use Your Data

Your email address is used solely to deliver the communications you requested. We will never sell, distribute, lease, or monetize your personal information to third parties.

Unsubscribing

You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any email you receive, or by emailing alysthorne@gmail.com directly.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please reach out via the contact page or by emailing alysthorne@gmail.com.
