Lore & World

Welcome to the bloodbound archive. Hidden histories, forgotten powers, and the rules that govern the shadows.

BlackspireBloodlines & MagicKey History
Blackspire

A metropolis veined with secrets. Vampires rule from high towers, but it's the alleys, archives, and hidden sanctuaries that truly pulse with danger.

The First House

The oldest and most powerful vampire lineage, obsessed with purity and control.

The Underground

A haven for rebels, rogues, and those who don't fit anywhere else. Home to bloodwitches and outcasts.

Neutral Zones

Fragile pockets where humans, lycans, and vampires coexist… barely. No one draws first blood here — unless they want war.

Bloodlines & Magic

In this world, blood is memory, and power is passed through the veins. The truth is written in what flows beneath your skin.

Bloodborn

Vampires born of vampire parents. Rare, revered, and feared. Said to carry the purest magic.

Moonblooded

Hybrids like Tessa. Human and lycan blood mingle with dormant power — until it awakens.

Blood Rites

Magical rituals fueled by sacrifice, memory, or ancient vows. Some bind. Some kill. Some remember.

Key History

Every wound the city carries was dealt by someone. Every version of the story depends on who survived to tell it.

The Culling

A systematic purge of lycans and rebels by the Council. Survivors were marked or erased.

The Broken Treaty

The shattered treaty between vampires and humans. No one remembers who broke it first — only the blood that followed.

The Rebellion

A whisper turned wildfire. Some say it failed. Others believe it simply changed form.
