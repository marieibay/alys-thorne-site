The World of Blackspire

Every city is built on something buried. Blackspire was built on blood.

Blackspire: The City of High Spires and Low Shadows

Blackspire is a vertical empire. Above the smog line, the vampire elite dwell in towering obsidian spires, ruling the city through wealth, blood tithes, and an iron-clad Council. Below, in the winding alleys and dripping tunnels, humans, dhampirs, and exiled vampires scrape out survival under constant surveillance.

Bloodlines & Forgotten Magic

In Blackspire, magic is not taught; it is inherited in the blood. Pureblood vampires wield shadows and dominate minds. But older, rarer bloodlines carry memories that span centuries — and the power to dismantle the spells holding the city together.

The Council

Seven ancient lords who maintain the masquerade and dictate blood law.

The Bloodbound

Those whose veins carry ancient oaths, bound to power or to doom.

The Hollow

The sunken district beneath the city where forgotten gods still whisper.

Key Historical Events

Centuries ago, the Pact of Veins ended the Blood Wars by dividing the city into strictly governed tiers. Now, with whispers of a living hybrid walking the streets, the ancient pact is fraying at the edges.
