Tessa Cole

The Hybrid · The Blood That Remembers

Tessa ColeThe Hybrid · The Blood That Remembers
The Hybrid. The Spark. The Blood That Remembers.

Once just a girl trying to survive the shadows of the city, Tessa Cole never asked for power — but it pulses beneath her skin like a curse. With blood that defies vampire, lycan, and human boundaries, she becomes both weapon and warning, hunted by the Council and haunted by a past she never knew.

Tessa's journey begins with survival — but becomes something far greater. She fights not just for her own life, but for those caught in the crossfire of prophecy, politics, and blood. Fierce, stubborn, and driven by an unshakable sense of right, she walks the line between rebellion and ruin, never forgetting what it means to choose your own fate.

Marked by magic. Bound by blood. Tessa doesn't just survive the system — she dares to break it.

Tessa & Isolde

Their story begins in blood and fear — but becomes something defiant, tender, and irrevocably true. Isolde is the shadow at Tessa's back. Tessa is the fire in Isolde's heart. Together, they rewrite the rules of loyalty, love, and legacy.

In a world where power corrupts and blood defines your worth, theirs is a bond born of choice, not fate. Every shared glance, every risk taken together, proves what neither dares to say aloud: they would burn the world for each other.
