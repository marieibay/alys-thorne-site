About Alys Thorne

Dark romantic fantasy, hidden histories, and rebellions that start with a whisper.

Alys Thorne writes dark romantic fantasy laced with gothic atmosphere, forbidden longing, and rebellions that start with a whisper.

A lifelong devotee of sharp-toothed myths, medieval folklore, and shadows that feel alive, she crafts stories where survival is messy, power is dangerous, and love refuses to stay silent. Her signature trilogy, Veins of Silver, was born from a desire to explore what happens when the lines between predator, protector, and beloved blur into ruin.

When she isn't writing in rain-drenched cafes or chronicling the cutthroat politics of the Vampire Council, Alys can be found collecting antique tarot decks, researching forgotten bloodline rites, or conversing with her companion animals.

“In this world, blood is memory. And love is defiance.” — Start reading Veins of Silver today.
