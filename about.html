About Alys Thorne

Dark romantic fantasy, hidden histories, and rebellions that start with a whisper.

Alys Thorne writes dark romantic fantasy laced with gothic atmosphere, forbidden longing, and rebellions that start with a whisper.

A lifelong lover of hidden histories and sharp-toothed myths, she crafts stories where survival is messy, power is dangerous, and love refuses to stay silent.

A forbidden bond. A city at war. Start reading Veins of Silver today.
