About Alys Thorne

Dark romantic fantasy, hidden histories, and rebellions that start with a whisper.

Download Author Photo (Press)
Alys Thorne is an author of dark romantic fantasy, gothic romantasy, and paranormal romance, best known for the completed trilogy Veins of Silver. Based in [PLACEHOLDER: where I'm based, e.g. London / the Pacific Northwest], she crafts atmospheric stories of blood magic, high-stakes political intrigue, and slow-burn sapphic romance.

A lifelong devotee of sharp-toothed myths, medieval folklore, and shadows that feel alive, she crafts stories where survival is messy, power is dangerous, and love refuses to stay silent. Her signature trilogy, Veins of Silver (comprising Blood Awakens, Shadows Unbound, and The Blood Remembers), was born from a desire to explore what happens when the lines between predator, protector, and beloved blur into ruin.

The series draws inspiration from [PLACEHOLDER: what inspired the series, e.g. gothic cathedral architecture, dark mythology, and historical bloodline rites]. In the towering, tiered city of Blackspire, seven vampire lords enforce a brutal blood tithe from obsidian spires, while human and dhampir rebels fight for survival in the catacombs below. At the heart of this fractured world is a slow-burn romance between Tessa Cole, an ordinary mortal whose dormant hybrid power awakens, and Isolde, a rogue vampire enforcer who chooses exile over massacre to protect her.

When she isn't writing in rain-drenched cafes or chronicling the cutthroat politics of the Vampire Council, Alys can be found collecting antique tarot decks, researching forgotten bloodline rites, or conversing with her companion animals.
