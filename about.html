About Alys Thorne

Dark romantic fantasy, hidden histories, and rebellions that start with a whisper.

Download Author Photo (Press)
Alys Thorne writes dark romantic fantasy laced with gothic atmosphere, forbidden longing, and rebellions that start with a whisper. A lifelong lover of hidden histories and sharp-toothed myths, she crafts stories where survival is messy, power is dangerous, and love refuses to stay silent.

Her signature work, the completed Veins of Silver trilogy (comprising Blood Awakens, Shadows Unbound, and The Blood Remembers), was born from an obsession with misty cobblestones, ancient blood covenants, and the intoxicating tension between natural enemies who become each other's salvation.

Set in the towering, rain-swept metropolis of Blackspire, the series pits human resilience and forbidden blood magic against the immortal tyranny of the Vampire Council. At its core is the slow-burn, open-door romance between Tessa Cole, an ordinary woman whose hybrid lineage marks her for death, and Isolde, a rogue vampire assassin whose blade turns against her own kind to keep Tessa alive.

When she isn't writing through the twilight hours or untangling the brutal political rivalries of the high spires, Alys can be found collecting antique folklore texts, studying historical occult rites, and hunting for quiet places where old stories still linger. She is currently writing her next dark fantasy saga, venturing into the cursed dynasties and wild sorceries beyond Blackspire's walls.

“In this world, blood is memory. And love is defiance.” — Start reading Veins of Silver today.
