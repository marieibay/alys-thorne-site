Blood Awakens is Book I of the completed dark romantic fantasy trilogy Veins of Silver by Alys Thorne.

Synopsis

Tessa Cole was supposed to be ordinary — until a violent encounter reveals her blood is anything but. Saved by Isolde, a rogue vampire with unreadable eyes and deadly grace, she's thrust into a world of secrets, shadows, and ancient power. As danger circles and her abilities awaken, the connection between them burns hotter than it should. But in a city where love between enemies is forbidden, desire may be her greatest threat.

First Lines & Excerpt

“You shouldn't exist,” the vampire said, rain cascading down the steel of her twin daggers. But she didn't strike. In the dark of the alley, her eyes caught the crimson pulse in my throat, and the world narrowed to the sound of our breathing.

“Then strike,” I challenged, my pulse pounding against my ribs like a trapped bird. “Or step aside and let me bleed in peace.” Isolde’s lips curved into something cold, beautiful, and utterly ruinous. “Neither,” she murmured, sheathing her steel with a faint ring. “Tonight, you belong to the shadows.”

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to read Blood Awakens first?

Yes. Blood Awakens is the foundational first installment of the continuous Veins of Silver trilogy. Reading the books in chronological order is essential to follow Tessa's awakening and her deepening bond with Isolde.

Is Blood Awakens a standalone story?

No. Blood Awakens concludes with a pivotal cliffhanger that leads directly into Book II, Shadows Unbound. The story arc and underground rebellion continue across all three books.

What is the romance heat and spice level?

The romance between Tessa and Isolde is a slow-burn sapphic enemies-to-lovers pairing with intense emotional tension, mutual danger, fiercely protective devotion, and open-door romantic chemistry.

Is the book available on Kindle Unlimited?
