Shadows Unbound is Book II of the completed dark romantic fantasy trilogy Veins of Silver by Alys Thorne.

Synopsis

On the run and hunted, Tessa and Isolde find shelter in the fractured underworld of the city — where danger lurks behind every promise and their growing bond could shatter everything. The deeper they fall into each other, the more the world pushes back. As bloodlines rise and magic stirs, love becomes not just a risk, but a rebellion.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to read Blood Awakens before Shadows Unbound?

Yes. Shadows Unbound picks up directly where Blood Awakens left off, following Tessa and Isolde as fugitives escaping the Council. The emotional stakes and world mythology require knowing the events of Book I.

Is Shadows Unbound a standalone?

No. Shadows Unbound is the middle volume of the continuous Veins of Silver trilogy and directly bridges the origin events with the climax in The Blood Remembers.

How does the romance develop in Book II?

The forced proximity of hiding together in the city's labyrinthine underworld deepens their bond from reluctant allies to fierce, protective devotion, escalating both emotional intimacy and high-stakes romantic passion.
