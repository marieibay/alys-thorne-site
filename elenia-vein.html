Elenia Vein

The Bloodbound Oracle

Elenia VeinThe Bloodbound Oracle
The Seer of Bloodlines. The Bearer of Forgotten Truths.

More myth than memory to most inhabitants of Blackspire, Elenia Vein walks the edges of the mortal and immortal worlds. As an oracle bound by ancient blood magic, her sight reaches back into the origins of the Vampire Council and forward into the ruins that await them.

When Tessa's hybrid nature awakens, it is Elenia whose prophecies begin to unravel the city's carefully enforced order. Quiet, calculating, and holding loyalties that answer only to cosmic balance, she provides the crucial wisdom Tessa and Isolde need to survive.

“The threads of fate were severed long ago. Now, we weave in the dark.”
